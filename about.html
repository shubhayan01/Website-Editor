Who We Are

A Practice Built on Personal Care

The Hearing Aid Doctor isn't a big-box hearing store. We're a locally owned, patient-first practice with three convenient locations across Southern Minnesota — in Lakeville, Northfield, and Stewartville.

Our specialists, Doug and Ariana, have spent decades helping patients find the right hearing solution. We work directly with manufacturer audiologists so every patient gets the most precise fitting, fine-tuning, and aftercare available.

We carry all major brands — Phonak, Oticon, Signia, Widex, Starkey, ReSound, and more — and we match the product to your hearing profile, not the other way around.
