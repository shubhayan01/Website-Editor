LLM.txt

Help AI crawlers understand your site

🤖 LLM.txt Content
File Content saved as /llm.txt on your server
ℹ️ What is LLM.txt?

LLM.txt is a proposed standard (like robots.txt) that tells AI language models how to understand and use your site's content. Place it at /llm.txt on your domain.

Add /llm.txt to your server root after downloading.
