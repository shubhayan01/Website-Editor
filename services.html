FAQ

Common Questions

How do I know if I need a hearing aid?
If you regularly ask people to repeat themselves, struggle to follow conversations in noisy environments, or turn the TV up louder than others prefer, a hearing evaluation is a great first step. Our evaluation is free and there's no obligation to purchase.
Do you accept insurance?
Many insurance plans, including Medicare Advantage plans, offer hearing aid benefits. We'll help you understand what's covered before you commit to anything.
How long do hearing aids last?
Most modern hearing aids last 5–7 years with proper care. We provide free cleanings and adjustments throughout the life of your device to keep them performing at their best.
Can I try hearing aids before buying?
Yes. We offer trial periods so you can experience improved hearing in your everyday environments before making a final decision.
